How it works

Every day at about 3:30 p.m. (Lisbon/UK time) the site posts tonight's games with two independent projections for every quarter and the latest injury news, and refreshes them at about 7:30 p.m. for late injury updates. At about 6 a.m. and 8 a.m. it checks the final scores and grades the night's picks.

  • Model: each team's offence and defence per quarter (recent games count more, only 40% of a team's own quarter pattern is kept because most of it is noise). If you enter the full-game total, the four quarters are scaled to add up to it. A quarter is a BET when the model gives it at least 55% to beat the line.
  • Classic: Henry's original method, unchanged: 3 H2H games + 2 games against a common opponent + season average, SD score (most consistent = 4) plus mean score (furthest from the line = 4). The highest total is the top pick.
  • Final picks: one per game, only where both methods point the same way. Strong = model BET and classic's top pick. Good = model BET and classic agrees. Lean = both agree, smaller edge. ⭐ Core = a final pick where the model is at least 1 point from the line; so far these have done much better than picks where the model sits on the line, so the Results page tracks them separately. ⚠️ = one of the teams is newly missing players worth 15+ points a game (from the injury box); the model can't see that, and those picks have done much worse. Best bets = ⭐ Core picks without ⚠️.
  • Injury impact: points per game of injured players. NEW absences played in the team's last 5 games, so the model's ratings still count them: the real total may be lower than the model thinks. Already out players have been missing for a while, so the ratings already reflect it. The book's game total includes all of this.
  • Skipped games: if the model's own full-game total is 8+ points away from the book's total, the book knows something (injuries, rest) and the game is left out. Check the injury report before betting.

This is the public, read-only copy. It refreshes about every hour in the evening and after the morning grading. Predictions are statistical and for information only. No outcome is guaranteed. 18+. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
